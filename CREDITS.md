## Data and external services

- **Occurrences:** [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/). Record popups link to original records. Underlying datasets retain their licences and citation requirements.
- **Baseline climate:** [WorldClim historical monthly weather](https://www.worldclim.org/data/monthlywth.html), CRU-TS 4.09 downscaled and bias corrected with WorldClim 2.1; custom provisional 2001–2020 preparation described in the README and app.
- **Future climate:** [WorldClim CMIP6](https://www.worldclim.org/data/cmip6/cmip6climate.html), SSP2-4.5, 2041–2060, three climate models listed in the README.
- **Satellite imagery:** Esri and the contributors credited in the live map. Attribution must remain visible.
- **Lake mask:** [Natural Earth 1:10m lakes and Europe supplement](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-lakes/), public domain, retrieved 22 October 2026.
- **Sweden boundary:** packaged as data/boundaries/sweden.geojson. Its original dataset and version still need to be documented before public release.
- **Species information:** [EPPO Global Database](https://gd.eppo.int/). Links provide further species information and photographs; no endorsement is implied.

## Scientific studies consulted

These studies informed biological interpretation or modelling choices. InvaSkog fits its own models; it does not reproduce all of these methods, and the study authors have not validated the app.

- Rossi, Mouttet, Rousse & Streito (2024). *Modelling the potential range of Agrilus planipennis in Europe according to current and future climate conditions.* Trees, Forests and People, 100559. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tfp.2024.100559
- Godefroid, Meurisse, Groenen, Kerdelhué & Rossi (2020). *Current and future distribution of the invasive oak processionary moth.* Biological Invasions 22, 523–534. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10530-019-02108-4
- Bentz et al. (2019). *Ips typographus and Dendroctonus ponderosae Models Project Thermal Suitability for Intra- and Inter-Continental Establishment in a Changing Climate.* Frontiers in Forests and Global Change 2:1. https://doi.org/10.3389/ffgc.2019.00001

The EAB study provides climate-modelling context; the OPM study's CLIM2 predictor set informed predictor selection. The MPB study provides mechanistic biological context; its physiological models are not implemented here.
